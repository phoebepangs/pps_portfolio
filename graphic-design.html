Graphic Design

01

LUUHKSOC Membership Card

Illustrator  ·  Print Design  ·  Branding

Physical membership card designed for Leeds University Union Hong Kong Society for the 2025–26 academic year, as Publicity Officer. A deep teal textured background references traditional Chinese craft papers; four red circular badges bearing 香港學會 (Hong Kong Society) in Chinese characters draw from classic HK branding. Gold letterpress-style typography and the society's logo complete the front face. Produced in CMYK at 300 DPI, printed as a physical credit-card format.

Instgram: @luhksoc that I was responsible for managing posts and announcements during this academic year, including the card reveal and event publishing.

02

Sérenade de Paris

Illustrator · Photoshop · Web Design · Campaign

A multiformat campaign for a fictional Paris fashion and music festival. Deliverables span a 4-page responsive website, an A4 event poster, three ticket tiers (General, VIP, Premium), merchandise (keychains, T-shirts, coasters), and web banners in different sizes. The handwritten "PARIS" wordmark replaces the "A" with an Eiffel Tower illustration, tying the campaign together visually.

03

The Decline of Cantonese

Illustrator  ·  Editorial Design  ·  Infographic

A design-for-social-change poster exploring the political, educational, and cultural forces behind the decline of Cantonese in Hong Kong. Built around a stylised map of HK with hand-illustrated landmarks — including a tram, minibus, and Victoria Harbour skyline — alongside archival-style stamps and text cards covering the 2014 Umbrella Movement, 2019 protests, and curriculum reforms. Produced in RGB at 1920 × 1080 px.

04

UpStage — Logo Redesign

Illustrator  ·  Brand Identity  ·  Print

A brand identity redesign for UpStage, a Leeds-based vintage clothing store with a theatrical, retro character. The new logo integrates a clothes hanger, pairing the Academy Engraved and Bodoni 72 Smallcaps typefaces. A warm cream base, forest green, and dusty rose create the vintage palette. Produced in CMYK at 300 DPI, with black-and-white variants for monotone print.
