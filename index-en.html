[ About Me ]

Welcome to Phoebe Pang's portfolio!

I'm a second-year BA Digital Media student at the University of Leeds. The course covers graphic design, interface design, programming, and media communications — but honestly, internships, part-time work, and personal projects have taught me just as much.

Video Production
Filmmaking is where I put most of my energy right now. In summer 2025 I had a social media management internship, and then moved into a remote part-time role as a video creator. That's where I started actually understanding visual rhythm, audience analysis, and what the data is telling you. I've also done modelling work in front of the camera, which gave me a feel for angles and composition that's hard to get otherwise - and it makes talking to photographers and crew a lot easier.

Graphic Design
I work mainly in Adobe Illustrator and Figma — brand visuals, social content, that kind of thing. Running an online store and serving as Publicity Officer for a university society meant design had to work under real constraints and real deadlines. That's where it actually clicked.。。

Programming & UI/UX
I've built up a foundation across HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, and PHP. For me, code is just another design tool. UI/UX shifted how I think — less "does this look good" and more "does this actually make sense for the person using it."

Beyond these, I've been dancing for years and competed and performed in quite a few shows. It might not be a career path, but that time on stage taught me a lot about discipline and working with a group that genuinely carry over into how I approach creative work.
Right now I'm looking for projects that let me use design, video, and programming together. Digital media keeps shifting, and I want to keep up with it. This is just a selection of my work, feel free to contact me to learn more!
