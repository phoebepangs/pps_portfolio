OIAD's marketing team reached out through Instagram to propose a gifted collaboration: products from their new lip collection in exchange for a short promotional Reel.

The entire production was handled independently — concept, filming, editing, and the caption. This inbound brand outreach — without an agency or manager — is a direct measure of content performance and audience trust built through consistent posts.

03

A Day in Hong Kong Vlog

Personal Vlog  ·  1 min
Vlog Editing Colour Grading Sound Design Personal Style Lifestyle

A one-minute personal vlog capturing a day out in Hong Kong — wandering the city, then heading to the sea. Shot and edited entirely in my own time, this piece is closest to my natural editing style, with a focus on capturing the essence of everyday moments.

Vlog editing is a different discipline from brand or social content — there's no brief, no client, no hook to engineer. The decisions are purely about feel: what to cut, what to let breathe, and how to make an ordinary afternoon worth watching for a minute.

Editing Note

"Vlog is more about recoring a vibe than telling a story. I wanted to capture the feeling of a casual day out — the little moments that make it special, without forcing a narrative arc."

A Day in HK  —  去看海的一天 Vlog · 1 min
04

SHOOT — Short Film MV

School Group Project  ·  Cinematography
Cinematography Video Editing Creative Direction Music Video Narrative Storytelling

A secondary school group project MV shot to ITZY's 《SHOOT!》 — a song about demanding clarity in love that doubles as a declaration of self-confidence.

The narrative follows a girl who is self-conscious, constantly envying the way other people seem to shine — until she decides to stop. The MV ends not with a love story but a resolution: everyone can be a Queen.

Camera angles were drawn from stage performance aesthetics to keep the energy grounded in the music.

Concept Note

"《SHOOT!》 is ostensibly about demanding an answer from an indecisive boy — but the confidence underneath belongs entirely to the girl. Stepping into your own light isn't about anyone else noticing."

SHOOT!  —  ITZY MV · School Project
